# Source provenance

The modules under `Cubical/Polytope` come from the polytope foundations in Caius Folkerts’ small nonrational four-polytopes formalization. They adapt Daniele Cappello’s SolarYs431/lean-eval-platonic-classification under Apache License 2.0. The files retain the original module-specific notices. The `Framework` modules extract the relevant parts of the same small nonrational four-polytopes formalization. Its support, homogeneous rank and realization transport proofs were adapted to the new fifteen-vertex certificate. The upstream notices are in `NOTICE` and the license is in `LICENSE`.

Lean and the pinned mathlib dependencies retain their respective upstream licenses.
